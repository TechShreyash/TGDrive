File-level immutable sources are recorded in MANIFEST.json with each included playbook's source URL, revision, blob and license. New-work also adapts the released mode-persuade.md, mode-operate.md and mode-read.md guidance; its additionalSources records their immutable paths and blobs under the same repository, revision and Apache-2.0 license. Uizze's reference/craft.md adapts upstream reference/craft-floor.md, not the deprecated craft command alias. Native audits use a separate audit.native.md playbook; an unavailable web scan is not evidence that a native interface passes. These are adaptations, not byte-identical upstream copies. The iOS source is mediated through Impeccable; retain the Platform Design Skills MIT notice in NOTICE and do not interpret the package's Apache-2.0 license as replacing it.
